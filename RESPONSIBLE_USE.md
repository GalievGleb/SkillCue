# Responsible Use

SkillCue is an interview-preparation product. It helps candidates study a specific vacancy, practice answers, and explain their real experience clearly.

## Use SkillCue to

- identify likely topics and knowledge gaps;
- practice answering in your own words;
- add real examples, outcomes, and evidence to answers;
- keep preparation notes organized;
- review AI output critically.

## Do not use SkillCue to

- invent qualifications, employment history, projects, or achievements;
- impersonate another person or substitute someone during an interview;
- present generated information as personal experience when it is not true;
- violate an employer's interview rules or assessment policy;
- expose another person's confidential information.

Candidates remain responsible for the accuracy of their answers and for following the rules of each interview.
